Your Emergency First Aid Companion

This project is an Android application designed to provide immediate first aid instructions and locate nearby hospitals during critical situations.

Features include:

  • Step-by-step first aid guides (Burns, Bleeding, CPR, etc.)
  • Real-time nearby hospital locator
  • One-tap SOS panic button
  • Bilingual support (English & Kannada)
